Full Rules

Every rule that governs the Challenge, from eligibility to scoring.

1Eligibility

Age19 to 65 years old
ResidencyResidents of China, Vietnam, or the 32 European countries listed below (27 EU member states, the United Kingdom, and 4 EFTA states)
Travel requirementWinners must be able to travel to Korea for the invitation programme
AccountEntries must be posted from a social media account registered in the entrant's own name. Private accounts are not accepted.
FollowersNo minimum

3Content Requirements

ProductA Korean YUJA product, obtained by the entrant locally
LengthShort-form video between 30 and 60 seconds
FormatAny style — drinks, desserts, cooking, everyday use. Entries should reflect the entrant's own ideas.
On-screen useThe video must show a Korean YUJA product actually being used. Simply showing the product on screen is discouraged.
Number of entriesUnlimited. Views and likes are totalled across all entries, and the number of entries earns bonus points.

4Posting Requirements

An entry that fails any one of these is excluded from scoring.

✓Include the hashtag #KoreaYujaChallenge
✓Tag the official account
✓Write YUJA or 유자 in the video or the caption
✓Post from a public account in your own name
✓Make the video between 30 and 60 seconds
✓Post between 14 and 28 September 2026

6Terms of Entry

By submitting, you agree to the following.

Keep entries public · Entries must remain publicly visible at least until scoring is complete. Entries that are deleted, set to private, or otherwise made inaccessible may be excluded from scoring.
Use of entries · The organisers may use entries on official social media accounts, this website, promotional videos, campaign materials, and reports. This includes reposting, editing, excerpting, translation, subtitling, and re-editing into short-form content. No additional fee is payable for such use.
Rights · All video, images, and music used in an entry must be created by the entrant or used with proper rights.

7Disqualification

Where any of the following is confirmed, the entry, or the entrant in full, is excluded from scoring.

· Artificially inflating results — buying views or likes, using bots or automation, or driving abnormal traffic
· Using another person's content without permission

10Ties and Counting

Where entrants are tied, rank is decided in the following order.

FirstHigher total views
SecondHigher total likes
ThirdMore entries
FourthHigher views on a single entry
FifthHigher likes on a single entry
Counting · Figures are fixed as of the start of scoring on 29 September 2026, and screenshots are kept as the record. Changes after that point are not reflected.
