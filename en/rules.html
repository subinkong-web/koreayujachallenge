Full Rules

Everything that governs the Challenge, from who can enter to how the draw works.

1Eligibility

ResidencyResidents of China, Hong Kong or Taiwan, Vietnam, or the 32 European countries (27 EU countries, the UK, 4 EFTA countries)
AccountAn SNS account in the entrant's own name (private accounts cannot take part)
FollowersNo minimum
Travel requirementWinners must be free of any condition that would prevent travel to Korea. If not, we draw again

3How to Join

Pick any video from our official feed and do all four.

1Follow the official account for your region
2Like the video you picked
3Leave a comment on the video you picked
4Share the video you picked
That's it — you're entered. Nothing to submit. The email submission step has been dropped.

4Sharing conditions — by platform

Sharing works differently on each platform. Follow the method for the platform you are using.

PlatformHow to share
TikTok · DouyinTap Repost
InstagramShare it to your Story and tag our official account
Instagram has no repost button. Share it to your Story and tag our official account so we can see it.

5What to write in your comment

Tell us what you think of Korean YUJA — what draws you to it, what you would like to try, what you are curious about.

"Looks delicious." "I want to try this." "I wonder how it smells." — Your own language is fine.
Abusive or promotional comments, or anything unrelated to the Challenge, will not be counted.

6Tickets and the draw

There is no judging panel. Winners are picked by random draw, with no scoring.

How you enteredTickets
Follow, like, comment and share (basic entry)1

Post a video and your 1 ticket becomes 100, so your chance of winning is 100 times higher. A video is optional — basic entry still counts without one.

7Video conditions — 100 tickets

A video that meets all of the following earns 100 tickets.

✓At least 30 seconds long
✓A Korean YUJA product must actually be used on screen
✓Posted on a public account in your own name
✓Include the hashtag #KoreaYujaChallenge
✓Posted between 14 September and 1 November 2026
✓No limit on how many you post

8Terms of Entry

By entering, you agree to the following.

Keep it public · Entries must stay public at least until the draw is complete. Content that is deleted, made private or otherwise unreachable may be excluded from the draw.
Use of entries · The organisers may use entries on official social media accounts, this website, promotional videos, campaign materials, and reports. This includes reposting, editing, excerpting, translation, subtitling, and re-editing into short-form content. No additional fee is payable for such use.
Rights · All video, images, and music used in an entry must be created by the entrant or used with proper rights.

9Disqualification

Where this is found, the entry or the entrant as a whole is excluded from the draw.

· Repeat entries through bots, automated tools or multiple accounts
· Buying views or likes, driving abnormal traffic, or any other artificial inflation of numbers
· Unauthorised use of someone else's content
