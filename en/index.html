RULES & ELIGIBILITY

Who can enter · Residents of China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Vietnam, or the 32 European countries listed. Private accounts cannot take part.
Use of content · Submitted videos may be used in promotion by the organisers.
Cheating · Repeat entries through bots or automated tools, or any artificial inflation of numbers, will be excluded from the draw.
Winner checks · Winners must send a screenshot of their share, a link to any video they posted, and passport details so we can book the flight. Passport and visa are the winner's own responsibility, and the prize cannot be exchanged for cash.
