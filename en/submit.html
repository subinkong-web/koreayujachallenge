1The submission step has been dropped

The way you enter the Challenge has changed, and the email submission step is gone. The comment you leave on a video in our official feed is your entry record. There is nothing for you to send us.

If you already submitted by email · Your email is kept on file. There is no need to send it again. You do, however, need to follow, like, comment and share as the new method requires for your entry to count.

4Share the video you picked
