How to Submit

Send the links to every video you posted, in one e-mail. Videos you don't submit are not scored.

1When to send

Submission period
Oct 3 – 5, 2026
Closes Oct 5, 23:59 KST (Korea time)
Where to send
koreayujachallenge
@gmail.com
Official entry e-mail
How many times
Once, all together
Put the links to all your videos in a single e-mail
You can't add links after the deadline. Post your last video first, then send everything at once.

2What to include

Five things. If any one is missing, checking your entry takes longer.

① Full nameYour own name
② SNS IDThe ID of the account you posted from
③ RegionChina, Vietnam, or Europe
④ Contact e-mailYour own e-mail, where we'll send the result
⑤ All video linksEvery video you posted, one link per line

3E-mail template

Copy this, fill it in, and send.

To
overmediaart@gmail.com
Subject: [KOREA YUJA CHALLENGE] Entry submission — Your Name 1. Full name: 2. Social media ID: 3. Region: China / Vietnam / Europe 4. Contact e-mail: 5. Links to every video posted 1) 2) 3) * I confirm that the account and the videos above are my own, and that they meet the posting requirements.

4Check before you send

✓Every video carries #KoreaYujaChallenge
✓You tagged the official account
✓YUJA or 유자 appears in the video or caption
✓Your account is public
✓Each video runs 30 to 60 seconds
✓No link is missing
Submitted videos must stay public until scoring is finished. Deleting a video or switching it to private may remove it from scoring.

5After you send

Oct 6 – 7
Scoring
We count views, likes, and entries. There is no jury
Oct 8
Winners announced
One per region, three in total. Announced on the official accounts and here
Trip to Korea
Flights · stay · YUJA cooking class
